# A kind is what a piece is: its drawing, the domain it moves in and how far it walks a turn (none for a piece that
# stays put), its contact's radius and the domains it reaches, and its body's radius, all in cells; and its traits,
# what it can do to a piece of another faction it walks to and what can be done to it there: a piece that Captures
# can conquer a Capturable one.
#
# Factions play in the order written, each with the colour what it holds is shown in, [r, g, b]. A piece belongs to
# the faction it is under and is in its culture, unless it gives its own. Where it stands, at, is [x, y] in cells from
# the top left corner of the world. A piece may be the capital of a region, capital_of, named by the region's id; a
# region has at most one, and each settlement is the capital of its own.
