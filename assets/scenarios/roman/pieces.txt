# Starting factions and pieces. Distances in cells; enum values written as declared (Large_City, Land).
#
# kind: name, icon, moves (domain; omit = static), per_turn (movement points), contact (radius), contact_on (domains),
#       body (radius), hindrance (enemy movement cost multiplier inside its contact zone, default 1), supply (supply
#       source value 0..100, default 0 = not a source), traits (Capturable changes hands when dissolved by a Captures
#       piece, and the army inside surrenders; Can_Enter can enter an empty Can_Contain of its faction; Fortified
#       fights sorties from its walls, as does an army inside it; Shares_Supply counts in nearby armies' supply
#       shares; Has_Town has a town economy: population and buildings, and a main building, which gives it its icon,
#       contact, body, hindrance and supply instead of its kind)
# faction: name, culture (by id, from cultures.txt), color [r, g, b] 0..255, treasury (starting coin), food_store
# (starting food in its central store, default 0), offices (its own offices, by id from offices.txt), capital (the
# name of one of its pieces, where its own offices' armies mobilise). Factions play in file order. Then one piece row
# per piece:
#   kind, name (optional), at [x, y] from top left, culture (default: faction's), capital_of (region id, max one per
#   region), title and of (an army's: the office whose army it is, by id from offices.txt, with an army_type; a
#   building's office also names its town, with of. Its holder leads it, with men, at 100% readiness, a local one at
#   home where it starts; its full strength follows its holder's wealth). A settlement's garrison is given instead as
#   men + proficiency 0..100, foraging 0..100 (army skill at living off the land, default 40) and mobility (1..4,
#   default 2)
#   population, main and buildings (a Has_Town's starting population; its main building, by id, one with the Main
#   flag, which sets its icon, slots, supply, hindrance and radii; and its other buildings, by id, at most its slots)
