# The kinds of office. Each has an id, a snake_case word the other files name it by, and a name. Then:
#   rank: its place in the order of offices; whoever is appointed rises to it
#   influence: what holding it gives
#   term: turns its holder serves before he can be promoted or dismissed
#   flags: Eternal, held for life; Dismissable, its holder can be dismissed once his term is served; Feudal, held as a
#   fief: dismissing the holder of one costs every holder of one in the faction loyalty
#   salary: a faction office's, coin per turn paid to its holder while held, out of the treasury. A building's office
#   is paid by its building instead
#   income_share, optional: a faction office's share of its faction's income, 0..1, paid to its holder while held,
#   out of the treasury, besides its salary
#   army_type, optional: the army its holder commands, by id from army_types.txt. Demobilised, the title gathers its
#   strength; mobilised, beside its town or, for a faction's own office, its capital, it takes the field
# Factions list in pieces.txt the offices they have of their own. Buildings name in buildings.txt the office each
# creates, which takes its town's name: Governor of Ravenna.
