# The kinds of building. Each has an id, a snake_case word the other files name it by, and a name. Then its effects,
# each 0 or none when left out:
#   population: the population it occupies
#   food: food per turn; food_fertility: food per turn, times its region's fertility
#   labour: labour per turn
#   income, upkeep: coin per turn into its owner's treasury, and out of it
#   control: radiated to its owner's towns, less their distance. A town sums what reaches it, up to 100
#   public_order: its town's public order, as given: positive keeps order, negative stirs unrest
#   food_store: food into its owner's central store, scaled by its town's control; negative, food taken out of it,
#   which adds no food to the town by itself
#   features: the local features it provides, written as declared (Horses, Drill, Court)
#   upgrade: the building it can be upgraded into, by id: its next level
#   build_labour, build_turns, build_coin: building it uses labour each turn for build_turns turns, and coin at the
#   start
#   description: what it is, shown when it is hovered
# A negative food or labour is consumed; a town using more labour than it makes runs a deficit. Income is scaled by its
# town's control.
