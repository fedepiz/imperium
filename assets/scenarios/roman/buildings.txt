# The kinds of building. Each has an id, a snake_case word the other files name it by, and a name. Then its effects,
# each 0 or none when left out:
#   population: the population it occupies
#   food: food per turn; food_fertility: food per turn, times its region's fertility
#   income, upkeep: coin per turn into its owner's treasury, and out of it
#   control: radiated to its owner's towns, less their distance. A town takes the strongest that reaches it
#   public_order: its town's public order, as given: positive keeps order, negative stirs unrest
#   food_store: food into its owner's central store, scaled by its town's control; negative, food taken out of it,
#   which adds no food to the town by itself
#   title_salary_global, title_salary_local: coin per turn paid to its title's holder while held, out of the treasury
#   (counted in its town's upkeep), and out of its town's local wealth
#   title_salary_local_share: its title's weight in the share of its town's local wealth left after local salaries
#   flags: Main, a main building: what kind of settlement its town is, how big and how governed. Built in the main
#   slot only, and nothing else is
#   icon, slots, supply, hindrance, contact, body: a main building's settlement, copied onto it: its map icon (Town,
#   City, Large_City), its ordinary slots, the supply it gives, the hindrance in its zone, its contact and body radius
#   title: the office it creates, by id from offices.txt, named after its town
#   upgrade: the building it can be upgraded into, by id: its next level
#   build_turns, build_coin: building it takes build_turns turns, and coin at the start
#   description: what it is, shown when it is hovered
# A negative food is consumed. Income is scaled by its town's control.
