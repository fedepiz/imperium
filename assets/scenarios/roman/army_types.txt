# The types of army. Each has an id, a snake_case word the other files name it by, and a name. Then:
#   scope: Local, tied to a home region, the one it is mobilised in; or Global, with none
#   men_per_wealth: its full strength, per coin a turn of its holder's wealth
#   proficiency 0..100, foraging 0..100, mobility 1..4: at full strength
#   description: what it is, shown when it is hovered
# Every key is given: none has a default. Titles raise them: the offices in offices.txt name their army types, and
# their holders' wealth, their salaries and shares, sizes them.
